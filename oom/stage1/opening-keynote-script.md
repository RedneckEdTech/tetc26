# THE MERLIN TRIALS: OPENING KEYNOTE SCRIPT & STAGING GUIDE
**Session Stage:** 0:00 – 0:10 (Opening Keynote Hook & Lore Reveal)  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 10 Minutes  
**Audience:** K–12 Educators & Instructional Leaders (Up to 48 Participants across 8 Tables of 6)  
**Presenter Persona:** The Headmaster / Chief Ministry Proctor  

---

## 1. Physical Room Staging & Pre-Session Setup Checklist
Before teachers enter the chamber, ensure the following physical and digital elements are primed:
* [ ] **The Order of Merlin Cup Trophy:** Placed prominently on a central wooden pedestal or table beside the projector screen. Flanked by satin spools of White (Third Class), Purple (Second Class), and Green (First Class) ribbons.
* [ ] **House Table Layout:** 8 round or rectangular tables preset for 6 seats each (2 Gryffindor, 2 Ravenclaw, 2 Hufflepuff, 2 Slytherin).
* [ ] **House Table Tents:** Placed at the center of each table displaying the House crest, motto, *"6 Wizards Max per House"* seat cap, and the QR code pointing to `stage1_muggle_studies.html`.
* [ ] **The Sealed Sorting Envelopes:** One 9"x12" clasp envelope per table, sealed with an official House sticker, containing:
  * 2 Six-Sided Dice (D6)
  * 1 Deck of blank 3"x5" index cards
  * 1 Pad of 3"x3" sticky notes
  * Fine-tip markers
  * 1 Marauder's Map Stage 1 Quest Checklist
* [ ] **Facilitator Pouch:** Loaded with physical +5 Point Hatstall Loyalty Tokens, sealed Keeper's Bonus Trial #1 Envelopes (+15 pts), and cut Felix Felicis ("Liquid Luck") Hint Cards.
* [ ] **AV / Screen Display:** Slide 1 projected with ambient wizarding orchestral music playing softly. Master 10:00 countdown timer queued.

---

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Verbatim Keynote Script

### [0:00 – 0:03] Act I: Welcome, Room Lore, and the Sorting Hat Rule
*(The presenter steps to the center of the room with theatrical warmth and quiet authority. Ambient music fades.)*

> **PRESENTER:**  
> "Welcome, scholars, educators, and fellow architects of learning, to the Department of Magical Education!
>
> Today, your everyday classroom reality is suspended. You have not simply stepped into another professional development session; you have entered the **Chamber of the Merlin Trials**. 
>
> Look around your tables. Before you stands your House banner—Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff, and Slytherin. For those of you still seeking a place, choose your House table. But take heed of the ancient **Sorting Hat Rule** inscribed upon your table tents: **strictly six wizards per table**. 
>
> If your first-choice House has already reached its six-wizard capacity, do not fret. As Albus Dumbledore reminded us, the Sorting Hat always places us where our brilliance is needed most! And should the Sorting Hat reassign you to a second-choice table today, step forward with pride: you immediately earn your new table a **+5 House Point 'Hatstall' Loyalty Bonus** to launch your team up the official ledger!"

*(Facilitator Action: Hand physical +5 point tokens to any participant displaced to balance tables. Ensure all 8 tables have exactly balanced seat counts.)*

---

### [0:03 – 0:06] Act II: The Order of Merlin Lore & Ribbon Progression
*(The presenter walks toward the pedestal, gesturing to the gleaming Cup and the three satin ribbons.)*

> **PRESENTER:**  
> "For centuries, the Ministry of Magic has bestowed its highest honor upon those who demonstrate extraordinary ingenuity, courage, and pedagogical mastery: **The Order of Merlin**.
>
> Today, your House tables are locked in a collaborative escape room challenge across three progressive chambers to earn your classification into the Order:
>
> * **Third Class (The White Ribbon):** Awarded for *Contributions to Knowledge* in **Muggle Studies**. In Hogwarts lore, Muggle Studies honors human ingenuity without magic. In our realm, this represents the foundational craft of unplugged pedagogy—building tabletop games using dice, cards, and rules that engage students even when the Wi-Fi collapses!
> * **Second Class (The Purple Ribbon):** Awarded for *Achievement Beyond the Ordinary* in **Transfiguration**. In our second chamber, you will take your physical game mechanics and use an AI Incantation in **Google Gemini Canvas** to transfigure analog cards into an interactive, standalone digital web game.
> * **First Class (The Green Ribbon):** Awarded for *Outstanding Bravery and Pedagogical Distinction* in **Charms**. In our final chamber, you will cast a binding spell—deploying your digital game live to the web via **GitHub** and bundling both offline directions and digital assets into a unified classroom release package.
>
> Every educator who conquers all three chambers will earn their official **Order of Merlin First Class Certificate**. But for the single House table that demonstrates the swiftest wit, deepest pedagogical insight, and amasses the highest score on my official ledger—you will hoist **The Order of Merlin Cup** as our grand champions!"

---

### [0:06 – 0:10] Act III: Table Kit Mechanics, Pacing Rules, and Trial Launch
*(The presenter picks up a sealed Sorting Envelope and holds up the two D6 dice.)*

> **PRESENTER:**  
> "Here are the chamber regulations for Stage 1:
>
> At the center of your table sits your sealed **Sorting Envelope**. Inside, you will find 2 six-sided dice, blank index cards, sticky notes, and your House’s **Marauder’s Map checklist**. You will also see a QR code on your table tent. Scanning that QR code or opening `stage1_muggle_studies.html` on your laptop launches your table's **Chamber Terminal**—a standalone tool that runs completely offline with zero internet dependencies.
>
> When the master clock strikes, you will have **exactly 10 minutes** to complete Stage 1:
>
> 1. **Role Delegation:** Appoint one designated **Scribe** to operate the terminal and prompt Google Gemini, while your fellow wizards divide and handwrite your **12 Challenge Cards** and **dice movement rules**.
> 2. **Speed Honors:** The first House to crack the chamber lock earns a **+10 Speed Bonus**, second place earns **+5**, and third earns **+3**.
> 3. **The Pacing Ladder:** If multiple Houses are stalled at the 5-minute mark, a room-wide **Professor's Guidance Hint** will appear on the main screen. At 3 minutes remaining, I will quietly deliver a vial of **Felix Felicis—Liquid Luck**—to any struggling table so no wizard is left behind!
> 4. **Fast Finishers:** If your team finishes early, wave me down to claim your **White Ribbons** and a sealed **Keeper’s Bonus Trial #1 Envelope** for an immediate **+15 House Points**!
>
> Scribes, open your terminals! Wizards, unseal your Sorting Envelopes!
>
> **Chamber Stage 1: Muggle Studies is now OPEN!**"

*(Facilitator Action: Trigger the 10:00 master countdown timer on Slide 5. Cue energetic, focused instrumental pacing music. Circulate room to observe table Scribes launching the terminal.)*
